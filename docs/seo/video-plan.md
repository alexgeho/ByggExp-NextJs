# YouTube Video Plan — tidrapportering cluster

**Owner shoots the videos. Claude embeds each on its target page + writes title/description.**
Source keyword list: 48 tidrapportering/tidredovisning/excel/app variants (2026-08-28).

## Rules (YouTube SEO, established)
- **One video per search intent, not per keyword.** 48 keywords → 6 videos. Swedish synonyms
  share a video; the video's title targets the head term, the page targets the exact keyword.
- **Swedish titles** (audience searches in Swedish; English won't match "tidrapportering app").
- **Format:** `[keyword ≤35 chars] — [hook] | ByggExp`. Keyword front (visible in search/suggested),
  hook drives CTR.
- **Every video → embedded on its commercial page** (dwell time + video-in-search). Owner sends the
  youtu.be URL, Claude embeds like the GPS video (`youtube-nocookie` responsive iframe).

## Prioritized shoot list

  projekt excel, tidrapport excel gratis, tidrapportering excel gratis, tidrapport excel 2021, mall

1 (Excel mall) → 2 (gratis app) → 3 (bygg) → 4 (bästa) → 5 (platform shorts) → 6 (optional competitor).

## Workflow per video
1. Owner shoots + uploads to YouTube (Swedish title from options above; A/B the two variants).
2. Owner sends the `youtu.be/…` URL.
3. Claude embeds it on the target page(s) (responsive youtube-nocookie iframe) + tightens the on-page
   copy around it, and updates domination-map.md.
